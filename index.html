Our Services

Manpower Supply

ABMS specializes in providing skilled, semi-skilled, and unskilled manpower to meet the demands of various industries, including construction, oil and gas, manufacturing, and infrastructure development. Our workforce is meticulously selected, trained, and certified to ensure they meet industry standards and client expectations. From engineers and technicians to laborers and safety officers, we provide the right talent to enhance productivity and efficiency on every project.

General Contracting

We offer end-to-end general contracting services, managing projects from inception to completion. Our expertise covers a wide range of sectors, including residential, commercial, and industrial construction. With a focus on quality, timely delivery, and cost- effectiveness, we handle project planning, design, procurement, execution, and commissioning. Our team of experienced professionals ensures that every project is completed to the highest standards, adhering to safety regulations and client specifications.

Industrial & Safety Material Supply

Safety and efficiency are paramount in any industrial operation. ABMS is a trusted supplier of high-quality industrial and safety materials, including personal protective equipment (PPE), tools, machinery, and consumables. We partner with reputable manufacturers to provide products that meet international safety standards. Our commitment to safety ensures that our clients can operate in a secure and compliant environment.

Logistics

Our logistics services are designed to optimize supply chain operations, ensuring the seamless movement of goods and materials. We offer comprehensive solutions, including transportation, warehousing, inventory management, and distribution. With a fleet of modern vehicles and a network of strategically located warehouses, we guarantee timely and secure delivery, enabling our clients to focus on their core business activities.

Heavy Equipment Rentals

ABMS provides a diverse range of heavy equipment for rent, including excavators, bulldozers, cranes, loaders, and more. Our fleet is well- maintained and equipped with the latest technology to ensure optimal performance. We offer flexible rental options to suit the needs of construction, infrastructure, and oil and gas projects. Our team of experts is available to provide technical support and ensure the smooth operation of equipment on-site.
